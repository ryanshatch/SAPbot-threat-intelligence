# Public research roadmap

This repository publishes reviewed blockchain threat research and corrections. The application and its internal data pipeline are maintained separately at [SAPbot.org](https://sapbot.org).

- Keep the [catalog](../CATALOG.md) and [wallet index](../wallets.md) aligned with published cases.
- Review each new case's primary evidence, network-specific identifiers, actor-control claims, victim and recovery boundaries, and uncertainty before publication.
- Document legacy CSV layouts and migrate them case by case without losing field meaning.
- Preserve dated corrections and source provenance in the case narrative and [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).
- Expand validation only where it can check a factual invariant without promoting provisional leads to confirmed labels.

The public research checks are described in [data format](data-model.md). Application deployment and operation are outside this repository.
